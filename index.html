fig. 02 — every byte beamed and checked
02Cross-platform · offlineFlutter · 2026

Beam Share

Fast file transfer between Android, iPhone and Windows, with no internet, no router and no compression.

Pick files, folders, photos or videos, show a QR code, and they beam straight across: in any direction between Android, iPhone and Windows. The sender makes its own private Wi-Fi, so it works on a train or a mountain. Original photos keep their quality, dates and location, and every byte is verified before it counts as done.

  • Own transfer protocol: parallel TLS streams, small files packed together, big files in 4–8 MB pieces
  • BLAKE3 on every piece and every file; only a damaged piece is re-sent, and a dropped connection resumes where it stopped
  • Tested with 1,003 files + 2.4 GB, two forced disconnects: 0 errors · ~118 MB/s encrypted
  • Native code on each platform: Kotlin Wi-Fi Direct, Swift peer-to-peer Wi-Fi between iPhones, C++/WinRT hotspot on Windows

fig. 08 — every point quotes the page
08AI · document analysisPython + React · 2026

TermGuard

Reads your rent agreement or offer letter and marks the lines to question before you sign.

Upload a PDF, even a phone scan. TermGuard splits it into clauses, checks each one against a written red-flag checklist, explains it in plain English, Hindi or Kannada, and lists what a fair agreement should say but yours doesn't. Click a finding and the exact line lights up on the page. It gives information, not legal advice.

  • Quote check: a finding is only shown if its quote is really in your document, so the AI can't make clauses up
  • 98% precision, 89% recall on 88 hand-labelled clauses, 0 uncited findings, failure cases documented
  • Private by default: names, Aadhaar, PAN and phones hidden before any AI call; files delete themselves after an hour

fig. 09 — the outage, minute by minute
09SRE · incident toolingPython + React · 2026

BlackBox

A flight recorder for outages: what broke, in what order, and the change that probably did it.

BlackBox pulls in logs, Alertmanager alerts, deploys and Slack chat, squeezes tens of thousands of log lines into the moments that matter, ranks the changes that might have caused the outage, and drafts a blameless postmortem where every sentence links to its evidence. A built-in chaos lab breaks a demo shop on purpose so every incident has a known answer.

  • 34,033 log lines → 23 timeline items with Drain template mining and z-score spike detection
  • Found the real trigger first in 49 of 50 injected incidents, each hiding two decoy deploys
  • Postmortem sentences cite event ids that are checked to exist: 100% evidence-linked

fig. 11 — one pothole, one dot, however many frames
11AI · computer visionYOLO11 + FastAPI + React · 2026

PotSpot

Ride with a phone on the handlebar, get back a map of every pothole on the road.

A fine-tuned YOLO11 model finds potholes in dashcam video, ByteTrack follows each one across frames so it is counted once, and the GPS track from the same ride puts it on the map with a size estimate and a blurred photo. Repeat rides merge into "seen 3×", and a PDF groups the worst potholes by road for the city corporation.

  • Fine-tuned YOLO11n reaches 0.62 mAP50 on held-out test photos, 0.86 recall on close potholes
  • Tracking turns 1,238 raw detections into 22 map dots with zero double counts on a ride with known answers
  • Caught that 57% of the dataset had near-twin photos and split by twin group so the test score is honest

fig. 12 — pay once for the same question
12AI · infrastructureFastAPI + scikit-learn + React · 2026

Tokenomy

A gateway that cuts a company's AI bill without touching its app.

Tokenomy sits between an app and its language models and speaks the OpenAI API, so switching to it is a one-line change. Repeated questions come back from a cache for free, reworded ones from a smart cache that compares meaning, and easy questions go to a small cheap model while hard ones go to a strong one. A dashboard shows spend, savings and every request.

  • Router picks the cheap or strong model with 96% accuracy on held-out prompts
  • Caught a trap: "to Delhi from Mumbai" and "from Delhi to Mumbai" score 0.992 similar; a key-facts guard cut wrong cache hits from 2 in 8 to 0
  • Works with the standard openai SDK, including streaming, with 25 offline tests and CI that builds the Docker image

fig. 13 — one long video in, ready-to-post shorts out
13AI · videoFastAPI + Whisper + Next.js · 2026

HypeSift AI

Paste a link or upload a long video, press AI Generate, get finished shorts ready to post.

HypeSift transcribes the video, measures loudness, motion and scene changes, and picks the moments that work on their own. Each clip is cut on a complete thought, reframed to vertical around the speaker's face, captioned word by word and given titles, descriptions and hashtags for six platforms. Every clip shows a 0–100 score with the reasons behind it, and a browser editor and a plain-English assistant let you change anything before you download.

  • A 2:53 video becomes 4 captioned, reframed shorts with post copy in 90 s on a laptop GPU
  • Scores explained across 7 dimensions; the speech model transcribes 115 s of audio in 4.5 s
  • 37 tests, including a full upload → AI Generate → MP4 download run, and it costs $0 to run: every model is local
