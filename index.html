fig. 02 — every byte beamed and checked
02Cross-platform · offlineFlutter · 2026

Beam Share

Fast file transfer between Android, iPhone and Windows, with no internet, no router and no compression.

Pick files, folders, photos or videos, show a QR code, and they beam straight across: in any direction between Android, iPhone and Windows. The sender makes its own private Wi-Fi, so it works on a train or a mountain. Original photos keep their quality, dates and location, and every byte is verified before it counts as done.

  • Own transfer protocol: parallel TLS streams, small files packed together, big files in 4–8 MB pieces
  • BLAKE3 on every piece and every file; only a damaged piece is re-sent, and a dropped connection resumes where it stopped
  • Tested with 1,003 files + 2.4 GB, two forced disconnects: 0 errors · ~118 MB/s encrypted
  • Native code on each platform: Kotlin Wi-Fi Direct, Swift peer-to-peer Wi-Fi between iPhones, C++/WinRT hotspot on Windows

CrowdMate

One QR code for crowded places: virtual queues and lost-person safety tags.

Scan a QR, get a live token, go get chai. If a child or elder gets separated, whoever finds them scans their tag and the family is alerted instantly, without either side seeing a phone number.

  • TypeScript sync engine: offline outbox, idempotent ops, backoff with jitter, heartbeats
  • Works on dropping networks: service worker, IndexedDB, ETag caching
  • 69 automated tests (Vitest + Playwright) including WCAG 2.2 AA scans

fig. 08 — every point quotes the page
08AI · document analysisPython + React · 2026

TermGuard

Reads your rent agreement or offer letter and marks the lines to question before you sign.

Upload a PDF, even a phone scan. TermGuard splits it into clauses, checks each one against a written red-flag checklist, explains it in plain English, Hindi or Kannada, and lists what a fair agreement should say but yours doesn't. Click a finding and the exact line lights up on the page. It gives information, not legal advice.

  • Quote check: a finding is only shown if its quote is really in your document, so the AI can't make clauses up
  • 98% precision, 89% recall on 88 hand-labelled clauses, 0 uncited findings, failure cases documented
  • Private by default: names, Aadhaar, PAN and phones hidden before any AI call; files delete themselves after an hour

fig. 09 — the outage, minute by minute
09SRE · incident toolingPython + React · 2026

BlackBox

A flight recorder for outages: what broke, in what order, and the change that probably did it.

BlackBox pulls in logs, Alertmanager alerts, deploys and Slack chat, squeezes tens of thousands of log lines into the moments that matter, ranks the changes that might have caused the outage, and drafts a blameless postmortem where every sentence links to its evidence. A built-in chaos lab breaks a demo shop on purpose so every incident has a known answer.

  • 34,033 log lines → 23 timeline items with Drain template mining and z-score spike detection
  • Found the real trigger first in 49 of 50 injected incidents, each hiding two decoy deploys
  • Postmortem sentences cite event ids that are checked to exist: 100% evidence-linked

fig. 10 — every change shows its reason
10AI · writing toolsMERN + Gemini · 2026

Humanize AI

A free QuillBot-style writing suite that rewrites sentence by sentence and tells you why.

Paste a stiff or AI-sounding draft and pick one of ten modes, from Natural and Academic to Shorten and Custom. Each changed sentence is marked; click it to see the reason and keep your original if you prefer it. Also a grammar checker, a summarizer, a "My voice" mode that copies your own style, and a Word rewriter with change requests and version history.

  • Edits the .docx itself, not a text copy: headings, lists, tables and the references section stay untouched
  • Change requests like "make the Methodology more formal" only touch that section, and every version is kept
  • Free where QuillBot's free plan stops: 1,500 words vs 125, 10 modes vs 2, compare modes side by side
