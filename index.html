fig. 02 — scan, wait, walk away
02PWA · real-time2026

CrowdMate

One QR code for crowded places: virtual queues and lost-person safety tags.

Scan a QR, get a live token, go get chai. If a child or elder gets separated, whoever finds them scans their tag and the family is alerted instantly, without either side seeing a phone number.

  • TypeScript sync engine: offline outbox, idempotent ops, backoff with jitter, heartbeats
  • Works on dropping networks: service worker, IndexedDB, ETag caching
  • 69 automated tests (Vitest + Playwright) including WCAG 2.2 AA scans
ReactTypeScriptNode.jsWebSocketIndexedDBVitestPlaywright

fig. 03 — one star per fact you approve
03Hackathon · AIFront-end lead · team of 4

Paroh

A privacy-first digital twin that learns a student only from what they approve.

Built from scratch at GATEWAYS 2026. The twin asks questions out loud, turns answers into candidate facts behind a consent gate, simulates "what if" choices with real numbers and speaks its advice. I owned the front end: the living line-art twin, the visual data stories and the voice UI.

  • Consent-first loop: every fact is approved before the twin learns it
  • Animated twin that mouths speech, plus heatmaps, fidelity and what-if views

A gaming store with a street-poster arcade look and a backend that can't be fooled.

Store, cart, checkout, order tracking, wishlist, reviews and a full admin dashboard. Every screen is animated, every price is checked on the server.

fig. 05 — the glass fills with the grade
05Machine learning2026

Qualify Milk

Predicts milk quality from seven lab readings, and refuses to fake its accuracy.

Move the sliders and the grade updates live with confidence and plain-language notes. The interesting part: 976 of the 1,059 rows were exact duplicates, so a normal split scored a fake 100%.

  • Compared 10 models three ways, including grouped CV on truly unseen samples
  • Honest best: ~88% on unseen data, instead of a leaky 100%
  • Flask app with accounts, saved history, confusion matrix and feature importance
